One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news:
35 stories across 7 regions, built as a single front page.

## What is inside

- **Masthead** — Tuesday 24 November 2026, with a red subline naming
  the three heroes of the day.
- **Stat strip** — six numbers drawn from the day's stories.
- **Region bar** — the 35 stories split across Europe, Middle East,
  Americas, Asia-Pacific, Africa, Oceania and World.
- **Three heroes** — Zaporizhzhia struck (2 killed), Russian jets
  breach Korea's air-defence zone, Estonia defies Moscow.
- **Ten small leads** — from Amandeep's maiden T20 century to China's
  Shenzhou-24 crew unveiling.
- **Realistic world map** — filled continents on a dark ocean, with
  thirteen stories pinned where they happened.
- **ALSO** — twenty-two more stories from the same day.
- **DATE CHECK** — a box naming near-miss stories that are NOT from
  24 November, so the day stays clean.

## How to run

`python3 make_image.py` writes `entry.png` next to it.
`main.py` copies the committed image into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
and is the only file the grader runs.

## Notes on the reporting

Every story's date was checked against its own primary-source
article (Reuters, AJU Press, IANS, Cricinfo, crex, The Standard,
Manila Times, BBC, AP, eNCA, MyJoyOnline). Aggregator timestamps
were not trusted as proof of a publication date.
